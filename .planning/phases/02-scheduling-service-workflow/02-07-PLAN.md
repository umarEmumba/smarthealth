Prove and harden every failure path of the booking saga (SCHED-08) and add the recovery mechanisms the design relies on (D-03 reaper, research G7 lost-trigger starter, D-19 verification bar).

Output: maintenance workflows/activities, idempotent schedule registration wired into the worker, committed workflow histories, and the compensation / maintenance / schedule / replay / HTTP-concurrency / worker-crash tests.
