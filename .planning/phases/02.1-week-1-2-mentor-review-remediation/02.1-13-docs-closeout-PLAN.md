Open risks and limitations that the documents must state (carried from planning):
- NFR targets are not measured on the local stack (Phase 3 observability); the full profiles are not claimed to pass locally.
- Audit entries after a committed step are non-fatal (an entry can be missing if Mongo is down; the transition is durable in `appointment_status_history`); the general fix is the Phase 3 transactional outbox.
- Schedule-change and reconciler work is polled (default 15 s and 60 s), not event-driven; waiting waitlist entries of a deactivated provider are not cleaned up.
- Cancel and reschedule billing steps that exhaust their bounded budget are still recorded as `*_incomplete:billing_pending` for an operator; only visit billing has an open-ended retry and a reconciler.
- `no_show` is set by the sweep only (no manual staff action); legacy visits created before Phase 2.1 settle their appointment as `not_checked_in`.
- Refresh tokens are single-use: a client must store the rotated token; access tokens issued before the change carry no `rjti` and revoke only themselves at logout.
- The e2e visit flow needs a slot inside the check-in window and skips within 40 minutes of clinic-local midnight.
