Scenarios selected by env `PROFILE`: `smoke` (constant-arrival-rate 5 per second for 30 s), `sustained` (rate and duration from nfr.json), `burst` (rate and duration from nfr.json) and `contention` (the contention_requests from nfr.json fired by that many VUs at the single contention slot with distinct Idempotency-Key headers and distinct patients; the iteration passes on 202 or 409 and `teardown()` reads the appointments for that slot and fails unless exactly one is active). In the booking profiles every iteration takes the next plan entry (`scenario.iterationInTest`), books it with a fresh `Idempotency-Key`, expects 202 only, and for about 10 percent of iterations polls `GET /api/appointments/{id}` every 500 ms for up to 30 s recording the elapsed time in a custom Trend `booking_confirm_seconds`; a share of iterations also issues `GET /api/slots` and `GET /api/appointments/{id}`. Tag requests by `endpoint` (`book`, `read_appointment`, `read_slots`) and define thresholds from nfr.json: book p95/p99, read p95/p99, `booking_confirm_seconds` p95/p99, and `http_req_failed` below `error_rate_max` for the booking endpoint (use `http.setResponseCallback(http.expectedStatuses(202))` in booking profiles and `expectedStatuses(202, 409)` in contention). No secret or token is logged.
