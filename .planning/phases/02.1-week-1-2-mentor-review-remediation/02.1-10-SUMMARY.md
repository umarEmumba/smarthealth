# Phase 02.1 Plan 10: Visit billing retries open-endedly, a reconciler settles the rest Summary

**VisitCompletedWorkflow's billing branch now retries without limit (capped 5-minute backoff, `BillingNotFinalizable` the only terminal answer) behind `workflow.patched("visit-billing-retry-v1")`, and a `visit-reconciler` Temporal Schedule settles any completed visit whose billing or appointment marker is still empty after a grace period by running the same idempotent step bodies under the workflow's own id, proven by a recorded two-marker history next to the frozen v1 and p21 histories.**

## Performance

- **Duration:** about 20 min (three regression runs of 3 to 4 minutes each, a Compose rebuild and a live reconciler check)
- **Tasks:** 3 of 3 (3 task commits plus 1 docstring follow-up)
- **Files:** 6 created, 10 modified

## Accomplishments

- The reviewed defect is closed on the Visit side (S4): a Billing outage longer than the old 10-minute budget no longer ends the workflow `billing = "skipped:activity_failed"` with the record stuck at `verified`. The billing activity keeps retrying (2 s, 4 s, ... then every 5 minutes), never grows history (31 attempts, one scheduled event, asserted) and still stops immediately on a business refusal.
- The change is patched: new runs record `visit-billing-retry-v1`; the frozen `visit_completed_v1.json` and `visit_completed_p21.json` still replay, and the newly recorded `visit_completed_billing_p21.json` carries both markers.
- The reconciler covers what the workflow cannot: a legacy `skipped` run, a terminated workflow, a lost trigger. `downstream.reconcile_unsettled` reads `ix_visits_unsettled`, and per visit and per open step calls the shared step body with `visit-completed-{visit_id}`, so Billing's idempotency key and the audit dedupe key equal the workflow's. Racing the workflow (or itself) produces one Billing transition and one audit entry per step.
- A failure is isolated per step and visit and logged with ids only (`visit.reconcile_step_failed`); the marker stays empty so the next pass retries; business refusals are terminal markers (`not_finalizable`, `not_checked_in`) and are never selected again.
- It runs live: the visit worker registers the Schedule, runs a start-up pass, and the Schedule re-settled a real visit within a minute (Billing unchanged, one `finalized` row).

## Open Risks and Notes for Downstream Plans

- **Scheduling analogue (open, for 02.1-11 and the phase summary):** cancel and reschedule billing steps in Scheduling still give up after a bounded budget and record `*_incomplete:billing_pending`; this plan covers visit billing only.
- **Reconciler fairness under a persistent single-record failure:** `list_unsettled` is oldest first with a limit of 100. More than 100 visits stuck for a retryable reason for a long time would starve newer ones; a Billing outage hits all visits equally so it is not an issue there. A per-visit backoff column would be the fix if it ever matters.
- **A retrying workflow and the reconciler can hit a sick Billing together** (the workflow's capped backoff plus one attempt per visit per minute after the 10-minute grace). Both are idempotent; load is bounded by the cap, the SKIP overlap and the per-attempt timeout.
- **Patch ids and fixtures (for any further VisitCompletedWorkflow change):** `run()` now evaluates `workflow.patched(BILLING_RETRY_PATCH_ID)` first, then the branches list, then `workflow.patched(VISIT_COMPLETE_APPOINTMENT_PATCH_ID)`; markers are recorded in that order and a test pins it. A further command-sequence change needs its OWN patch id and a newly recorded history with a distinct name; never re-record `visit_completed_v1.json`, `visit_completed_p21.json` or `visit_completed_billing_p21.json`.
- **Stub-based workflow tests** still register four stubs (`_run(env, billing, followup, analytics, appointment)`); a billing stub that always fails now never ends, so bound the failure count.
- **Test pattern:** reconcile tests run `downstream.reconcile_unsettled(..., grace_minutes=0)` on freshly completed visits (seed `downstream_started_at` with SQL) rather than ageing rows, because the Compose visit worker runs a live 10-minute-grace reconciler against the same databases. Helpers `_stuck_visit`, `_reconcile`, `_unsettled_ids` live in `tests/visit/test_visit_reconciler.py`.
- **Compose:** the stack runs images built from this branch (visit and visit-worker rebuilt in this plan; scheduling and scheduling-worker from 02.1-09). `visit-reconciler` is live (not paused); the poller `scheduling-schedule-change-poller` is still paused on purpose.
- **Docs (plan 02.1-13):** the Visit event flow should add the reconciler Schedule and the open-ended billing retry; no schema change in this plan (the 02.1-09 migration already carries the columns and index).
