# Phase 02.1 Plan 09: Visit completion completes the appointment Summary

**VisitCompletedWorkflow gains a patched fourth concurrent branch that tells Scheduling the visit is over (appointment checked_in -> completed) under the long compensation retry budget, and billing finalize and appointment completion each leave a durable, conditionally written settlement marker in visit_db (a business refusal such as a never-checked-in legacy appointment is a terminal marker, never an endless retry), proven by a recorded p21 history next to the still-replaying v1 history.**

## Performance

- **Duration:** about 41 min (includes three full-suite runs of 2 to 4 minutes each and a Compose rebuild)
- **Tasks:** 3 of 3 (4 task commits plus 1 follow-up test commit)
- **Files:** 3 created, 10 modified

## Accomplishments

- Completing a visit now completes its appointment. The visit transition still commits and returns 200 first; the workflow's fourth branch calls Scheduling's internal complete route with the Visit service token, so the appointment reaches its terminal `completed` state and the slot stays booked for good (M1, VISIT-04).
- The new branch is concurrent with billing and the two intents (not sequenced after them) and uses `COMPENSATION_ACTIVITY`; a test asserts all four activities are scheduled in the same workflow task and that billing failing all 20 attempts does not delay or block the appointment branch.
- Settlement markers (S4 groundwork for plan 02.1-10): `billing_settled_at/_outcome` and `appointment_settled_at/_outcome` on `visits`, written by a conditional UPDATE so a retry or reconciler call never overwrites, plus the partial index `ix_visits_unsettled` for the reconciler's scan.
- Business refusals are terminal and recorded: `not_checked_in` (legacy visit), `no_appointment` (404, including another clinic's appointment), `not_finalizable` and `no_record` for billing.
- Replay safety: `workflow.patched("visit-complete-appointment-v1")`; the frozen v1 history replays, the new p21 history (marker plus four activities) replays, and the unpatched edit demonstrably fails replay.

## Next Phase Readiness (notes for plan 02.1-10, which patches this workflow next)

- `VisitCompletedWorkflow.run` now ends with `branches` + `workflow.patched(VISIT_COMPLETE_APPOINTMENT_PATCH_ID)` + `asyncio.gather(*branches)`; the results are read by position (`results[0..3]`). Any further command-sequence change needs its OWN patch id, a new `*_p21`-style recorded history (name it distinctly, e.g. by the new patch), and an "unpatched edit fails replay" test; keep `visit_completed_v1.json` and `visit_completed_p21.json` replaying. Do not re-record either of them.
- Stub-based workflow tests register FOUR stubs now (`_run(env, billing, followup, analytics, appointment)`); a fifth activity needs a fifth stub in every test.
- The markers are the reconciler's input: `ix_visits_unsettled` covers `status = 'completed' AND (billing_settled_at IS NULL OR appointment_settled_at IS NULL)`. Terminal outcomes already carry a marker (`not_finalizable`, `no_record`, `no_appointment`, `not_checked_in`), so "unsettled" means "still retryable or never ran". There are 4 older completed visits in the dev database with both markers NULL (not backfilled; they will settle as `not_checked_in` for the appointment, and billing depends on their record state).
- `mark_*_settled` are conditional and flush only: the activity's `_settle` commits them in its own short session. A reconciler must call the same idempotent activity logic (or the repo functions) rather than UPDATE directly, so a repeat never overwrites.
- Activity retry semantics: `AppointmentNotCheckedIn` and `BillingNotFinalizable` are raised `non_retryable=True`; everything else (transport, 401/403/5xx, token minting) propagates and Temporal retries within `COMPENSATION_ACTIVITY` (20 attempts / 10 min). After that budget the branch reports `skipped:<ErrorType>` with the marker still NULL: this is the case the reconciler exists for.
- Test helper: `tests/visit/test_downstream.py` exposes `_activities(http, mongo)`, `_run_activity(method, visit_id, appointment_id, vw)`, `_markers(visit_id)` and `_legacy_completed_visit(vw, appointment_id)`.
- The Compose stack now runs images built from this branch (visit, visit-worker, scheduling, scheduling-worker); the poller Schedule `scheduling-schedule-change-poller` is still paused.
