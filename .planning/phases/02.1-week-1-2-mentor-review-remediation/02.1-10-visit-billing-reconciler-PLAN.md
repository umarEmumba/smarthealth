<objective>
Visit-side half of S4 ("stuck states"): visit billing that is "skipped" and never retried. Plan 02.1-09 added durable settlement markers; this plan uses them to make billing finalization keep retrying and to reconcile anything that still slipped through.

Claim verification (done while planning, reproducible): `VisitCompletedWorkflow._branch` catches the billing activity's `ActivityError` and returns `skipped:<reason>`; with `COMPENSATION_ACTIVITY` (20 attempts, 10-minute schedule-to-close) a Billing outage longer than about ten minutes ends the workflow Completed with `billing = "skipped:activity_failed"`, and no other component ever finalizes that visit's billing record, which stays `verified`. The claim is real.

Design (planner discretion, called out): (1) the billing branch gets its own activity preset with unlimited attempts and a backoff capped at five minutes, behind `workflow.patched`; Temporal retries do not add history events, so an open-ended retry is cheap, and a terminal business answer (`BillingNotFinalizable`) still ends the step; (2) a reconciler Schedule in the visit worker (every 60 s by default, overlap SKIP) settles completed visits whose markers are still empty after a grace period, covering workflows that completed `skipped` before this change, were terminated, or never ran their branch; (3) the reconciler is a Schedule with a workflow of its own (a new workflow type, so it needs no patch), consistent with the sweeper pattern from plan 02-11; (4) the unlimited-retry policy lives in a Visit-local module so it does not collide with the shared policy module used by the Scheduling plans.

Scope note (called out): cancel and reschedule billing steps in Scheduling also give up after a bounded budget and record `*_incomplete:billing_pending` in the appointment history plus an audit entry; the review's bullet and CONTEXT name visit billing, so this plan covers visit billing and the phase summary lists the Scheduling analogue as an open risk.

Output: the preset, the patched billing branch, shared settlement helpers, the reconciler (function, activity, workflow, Schedule, worker wiring), and proofs with a recorded history.
</objective>
