# Phase 02.1 Plan 06: Visit Check-in Integration Summary

**Visit's public check-in now calls Scheduling's internal check-in first (aud=scheduling service token, window and cancel race arbitrated there) and only then inserts the visit, so after a 201 the appointment is `checked_in` and cancel and reschedule are refused; the two-service sequence converges on retry with no compensating call, and the real-stack visit flow was re-run against a rebuilt Compose stack.**

## Performance

- **Duration:** about 55 min (includes a Compose rebuild and two long test runs)
- **Tasks:** 3 of 3 (3 task commits)
- **Files:** 1 created, 5 modified

## Accomplishments

- **Claim verified (as planned).** Before this plan `check_in` read the appointment, required `confirmed`, inserted the visit and returned; nothing was written to Scheduling. The mentor's claim held.
- **Reference client.** `check_in_appointment` mints the aud=scheduling token, POSTs `/internal/appointments/{id}/check-in?clinic_id=`, returns the `AppointmentRef` dict, returns `None` on 404 and raises `CheckInRefused(detail)` on 409. Transport and auth failures propagate as `UpstreamServiceUnavailable` / `ServiceTokenUnavailable`. The caller's JWT is never forwarded (T-02.1-21).
- **Route.** Clinic-scope resolution and the existing-visit short-circuit are unchanged (an existing visit is a local 200 and never asks Scheduling). Otherwise Scheduling's transition runs first; `CheckInRefused` becomes 409 with one of three allow-listed details (anything else is `appointment not confirmed`, T-02.1-23), 404 stays the identical 404, outages stay 503 with nothing written. The visit insert, commit, 201/200 and `visit.check_in` audit follow as before.
- **Retry convergence (T-02.1-22).** If the visit insert (or the process) fails after Scheduling moved the appointment, the appointment is `checked_in` with no visit. The repeat gets 200 `already` from Scheduling and creates the visit. Proved three ways: a direct Scheduling check-in followed by the public call, an injected `create_visit` failure in the real route, and 20 concurrent requests.
- **Test harness.** `widened_checkin_window()` (opening window raised to 10,000,000 minutes on Scheduling's settings object, which the in-process Scheduling handler reads at request time) is applied to every test under `tests/visit` by an autouse fixture; a test that requests the no-op `real_checkin_window` fixture sees the real 60-minute window. No new pytest marker was registered.
- **e2e.** `book_slot_inside_checkin_window` creates a second provider in the world's department, one weekly template for today's weekday whose window is the next half-hour grid slot at least 5 minutes ahead, generates today's slots, and returns the slot and provider ids; it skips with an explicit reason within 40 minutes of clinic-local midnight (or if the slot would reach midnight). The visit lifecycle test books that slot, additionally asserts the appointment is `checked_in` and a cancel is 409, and lets the front desk start and complete the visit.

- **e2e depends on the clock.** `test_visit_lifecycle_finalizes_billing` skips within 40 minutes of midnight in Asia/Karachi; it passed in this run, so it did not skip.
- **Audit after commit.** Scheduling's `appointment.check_in` audit is written after its commit (plan 01's note). If Mongo is down at that moment the public check-in returns 500 after the transition applied, and the repeat then creates the visit but the Scheduling audit entry is never written. The S3 plan (audit failures non-fatal or outboxed) should cover this route and Visit's own `visit.check_in`.
- **Orphan checked_in appointment.** If the patient never retries after a mid-sequence failure, the appointment stays `checked_in` with no visit; no sweeper reconciles it. Plan 02.1-11 (Scheduling reconciler) is the natural owner.
